# Red, White, and Blue Trife

_Cuisine:  American_<br />
_Course:  Dessert_

## Ingredients

- 8 oz package cream cheese, room temperature
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1.5 cups heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 lbs pound cake, cubed
- 12 oz blueberries, about 2 cups
- 12 oz blackberries, about 2.5 cups
- 1.5 qt strawberries, sliced

## Instructions

1. Wash and prepar the **fruit**.
1. Cube the **pound cake** and set aside.
1. Combine **cream cheese** and **granulated sugar** in stand mixer.  Beat on medium-high speed until smooth, 1-2 minutes.
1. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and add the **heavy cream** and **vanilla extract**.  Switch to the whisk attachment and whip at medium speed until soft peaks form and the mixture is easy to dollop.
1. Place a single layer of **cake cubes** on the bottom of a large (12-14 cup) trifle dish.
1. Top with 2/3rds of the **blueberries and blackberries**.
1. Add half of the **whipped cream**, about 2 cups.
1. Then top with 2/3rds of the **strawberries**.
1. Repeat the **cake** and **cream** layers once more, then decorate the top with the **remaining berries**.
1. Store in the refrigerator for up to 24 hours.
