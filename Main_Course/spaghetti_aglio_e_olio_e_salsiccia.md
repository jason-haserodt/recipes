# Spaghetti Aglio e Olio e Salsiccia

_Cuisine:  Italian_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 1 lbs dried spaghetti
- 4 Tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lbs loose sweet italian sausage
- 6-8 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp red chili flakes
- 1 cup finely grated parmesan cheese
- 3/4 cup finely chopped parsley, plus more for garnish
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Mince the **garlic** and **parsley** and set aside.
1. Add **dried spaghetti** to a large skillet.  Cover with water, filling about 2/3 of the way full.
1. Add several pinches of **salt** and apply medium heat.
1. While the pasta water heats, brown **sausage** with **2 Tbsp olive oil** in a large pot over medium-high heat.
1. Once browned, removed **cooked sausage** to a plate.
1. Cook **pasta** for 2-3 minutes in boiling water, then turn off the heat.
1. Add **2 Tbsp olive oil** to the large pot over medium-low heat.
1. Add **minced garlic** and saute for 3 minutes.
1. Add **chili flakes** and saute for 30 seconds.
1. Transfer **2.25 cups pasta water** from the skillet to the pot and scrape up any burned bits.
1. Turn heat up to medium-high and reduce **liquid** by half, about 4-5 minutes, then turn heat to low.
1. Transfer the **cooked pasta** to the pot, add the **grated parmesian**, **parsley**, **cooked sausage**, and **salt**.
1. Toss over low heat until the cheese melts and an **emulsified sauce** coats all of the noodles.
1. Taste and add more **salt** if necessary.
1. Serve immediately and garnish with more **parsley** and **parmesian cheese**.
