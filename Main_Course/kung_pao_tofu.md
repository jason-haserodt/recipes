# Kung Pao Tofu

_Cuisine:  Chinese_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 1 14oz package extra-firm tofu
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- 2 Tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice wine or white white
- 1 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, pressed
- 1 1" piece fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp corn starch
- sunflower seed oil
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1/2" squares
- 2 celery stalks, cut into 1/4" slices
- 4 scallions, cut into 1" chunks
- 1 tsp sichuan peppercorns
- crushed red chili peppers, to taste
- 1/3 cup roasted peanuts
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves and stems, roughly chopped
- white rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Drain the **tofu**, wrap in a paper towel, set on a plate, and place a cast-iron skillet on top.  Let it press for at least 1- minutes and up to 1 hour.
1. While the tofu is pressed, pepare the **sauce**.
1. In a medium bowl, whisk the **soy sauce**, **brown sugar**, **hoisin**, **wine**, **vinegar**, **sesame oil**, **pepper**, **garlic**, **ginger**, 2 tsp **cornstarch**, and 1/2 cup **water** until combined.
1. Cut **red bell pepper**, **celery**, and **scallions** and set aside.
1. Roughly chop the **cilantro** and set aside as well.
1. After **tofu** has pressed, unwrap it and cut into 3/4" cubes.
1. Place **tofu cubes** in a medium bowl, season with **salt**, and coat in 1/4 cup **cornstarch**.  Set aside.
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, add enough **oil** to coat the bottom of the pan.
1. When the **oil** is shimmering, add the **coated tofu**.
1. Cook until one side is golden brown and crisp, about 3 minutes, then flip.
1. Cook for another 3 minutes, then remove with a slotted spoon and transfer to a plate.
1. Add the **red bell pepper**, **celery**, and **scallions**.  Stir occassionally until the red bell pepper starts to soften while maintaining some bite and the vegetables char, about 4 minutes.
1. Add the **sichuan peppercorns** and **chilis** and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
1. Add the **crisp tofu** and **sauce**, and stir to coat.  Cook until sauce thickens, about 2 minutes.
1. Stir in the **peanuts** and **cilantro**.
1. Serve immediately over **cooked rice**.
