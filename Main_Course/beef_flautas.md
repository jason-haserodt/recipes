# Beef Flautas with Buttermilk-Avacado Crema

_Cuisine:  Mexican_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 1 lbs loose chorizo
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lbs ground beef
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 12 6" flour tortillas
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1/3 cup sunflower seed oil
- 1 ripe avacado, chopped
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup buttermilk
- 1 tbsp lime juice

## Instructions

1. Chop **onion**, **cilantro**, and mince the **garlic**.  Set aside.
1. Crumble **chorizo** in a large skillet and cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes.
1. Stir in **onion** and **garlic**.  Cook until tender, about 3 minutes.
1. Add **ground beef**, **chili powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt**.  Cook, stirring often, until beef is browned and done, about 5 minutes.
1. Remove from heat and stir in the **cilantro**.
1. Divide the **beef mixuture** evenly between the **tortillas**, about 1/4 cup per tortilla.
1. Roll **tortilla** over the filling and brush edge with **beaten egg** to seal.
1. Heat **oil** in a large skillet over medium heat and fry **flautas** in batches, about 3-4 minutes per side.
1. Place **fried flautas** on a paper towel lined baking sheet.
1. Meanwhile, process **avacado**, **sour cream**, **buttermilk**, **lime juice**, and the remaining **1/2 tsp salt** in a food processor until smooth.
1. Serve **flautas** with the **crema** and a salad.
