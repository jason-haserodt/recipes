# Pork Chops in Lemon-Caper Sauce

_Cuisine:  American_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 4 pork chops
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 Tbsp butter, divided
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp flour
- 1 cup white wine
- 1.5 cups chicken stock
- 2 Tbsp capers
- 2 Tbsp parsley, minced, plus more to garnish
- 1 tsp lemon zest, plus 2 Tbsp lemon juice (1 lemon)
- A few dashes hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Mince **garlic** and **shallots** and set aside.
1. Chop **parsley** and set aside.
1. Zest **lemon** and set aside, reserving the lemon to juice.
1. Season **pork chops** aggressively with **salt**, **pepper**, and **thyme**.
1. Heat a skillet on medium heat and add **olive oil**.
1. Add **pork chops** and brown until cooked through, 5 minutes per side.
1. Transfer **pork chops** to a plate and cover to keep warm.
1. Add 2 Tbsp **butter** and then the **shallots** and **garlic** and saute until aromatic, about 1 minute.
1. Sprinkle in **flour** and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes.
1. Whisk in **wine** and **chicken stock**, raise heat to high, and bring the liquid to a boil, scraping up the browned bits on the bottom of the pan.
1. Reduce heat to medium-high and cook, uncovered, until the liquid is reduced by at least half, 7-10 minutes.
1. Stir in the **capers**, **parsley**, **lemon zest**, **lemon juice**, and **hot sauce** to taste and simmer for 1-2 minutes.
1. Stir in the remaining 2 Tbsp **butter** until it's melted.
1. Nestle the **pork chops** into the sauce and allow to warm up for several minutes.
1. Serve, pouring **sauce** over each **pork chop**.
1. Garnish with fresh **parsley**.
