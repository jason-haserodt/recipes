# Chickpeas al Limone with Burrata

_Cuisine:  Italian_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 5 Tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 (15oz) cans chickpeas, undrained
- 1 Tbsp lemon zest (1 lemon)
- 1/4 cup lemon juice (2 lemons)
- 1 cup finely grated parmesan cheese, plus more to top
- salt and pepper
- 8 ounces burrata, drained
- handful basil leaves
- sourdough or baguette, for serving

## Instructions

1. Mince **garlic** and zest **lemon** and set aside.
1. Place **parmesan**, 1/4 cup **olive oil**, and **lemon juice** in a large bowl.  Set aside.
1. Heat a deep, medium skillet over medium-high heat.
1. When hot, drizzle with 1 Tbsp **olive oil**, add **garlic** and cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds.
1. Stir in the **chickpeas** and their **brine**, then bring to a boil.
1. Reduce until thickened, about 8-10 minutes.
1. Reduce heat to low and add **lemon zest**.  Stir for 1 minute.
1. Add the **chickpeas and reduced brine** to the **parmesan-lemon mixture** and stir continuously until the cheese has melted and the sauce is thick but loose.
1. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.
1. To serve, divide among bowls and drizzle with more **olive oil**.  Tear the **burrata** over the top and sprinkle with the **basil** and more **parmesan**.
1. Serve with **bread**.
