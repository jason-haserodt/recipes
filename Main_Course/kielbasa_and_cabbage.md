# Kielbasa and Cabbage

_Cuisine:  Polish_<br />
_Course:  Main Course_

## Ingredients

- 14-16 oz kielbasa, cut into 1/4" coins
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1 medium yellow onion, halved and sliced into 1/2" strips
- 2 garlic cloves
- salt and pepper
- crushed red pepper
- 1 medium cabbage (2.5 lbs), cut into quarters, cored, and slice into 1/2" thick strips
- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 Tbsp dijon mustard, plus more for serving
- chopped parsley for serving

## Instructions

1. Quarter, core, and slice **cabbage**.  Set aside.
1. Slice **onion**, mince **garlic**, and chop **parsley**.  Set aside.
1. Slice **kielbasa** and set aside.
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
1. Add a bit of **oil** and **siced kielbasa** and cook until golden brown and slightly charred, about 3 minutes on each side.
1. Add **butter**, **garlic**, 1 tsp **salt**, 1/2 tsp **black pepper**, and a pinch of **crushed red pepper**, and cook, stirring, until the onion is starting to brown and garlic is fragrant, about 3 minutes.
1. Add **cabbage** and cook, stirring occassionally, until most of the cabbage has softened and browned, 10-12 minutes.
1. Add the **vinegar** and **mustard** and stir to incorporate, cooking for 1 minute longer.
1. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.
1. Garnish with **chooped parsley** and serve immediately with more **mustard** on the side.
